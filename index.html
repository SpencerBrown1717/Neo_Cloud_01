      Method and sources

      How the numbers are built, and where they come from.

      How we calculate

      Live market value is the latest trade price multiplied by shares outstanding, using the share count implied by each company's Sep 25, 2026 close. Year-end values are the market capitalisation on the last trading day of each year.

      Live prices come from Yahoo Finance and refresh every 15 seconds while the US market is open. When the live feed is not available, the page falls back to a copy refreshed every 15 minutes, then to the Sep 25, 2026 snapshot. The badge in the header always says which one you are seeing.
